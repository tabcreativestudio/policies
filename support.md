## Common Questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → Thrift Pro → Cancel Subscription.

**How does the AI scanner work?**
Point your camera at any clothing item and tap scan. Our AI identifies the brand, style, and condition, then estimates its resale value.

**Why is my scan not working?**
Make sure you have a clear photo of the clothing item with good lighting. The scanner works best with individual items, not people or non-clothing objects.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open Thrift → Profile → Settings → Delete Account.

**How do I change my username?**
Open Thrift → Profile → Settings → tap your username. You can change it once.
